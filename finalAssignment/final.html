Meet The Ghouls!

Frankie Stein

Frankie is the daughter of Frankenstein's monster. She is just 15 days old and she's friendly, kindhearted, and eager to make friends! She can be clumsy and sometimes struggles to understand everyday social situations at her new school, Monster High. She loves to sew and prartice science experiments when she is not in fearleading (cheerleading) practice.

Draculaura

Draculaura is the adopted daughter of Dracula! She is 1,600 years old and loves everything pink and sweet. She's terrified of blood, so she is strictly a vegan. She even faints at the sight of meat. She's naive and sweet and very much cars about her friends. When not spending time with her werewolf boyfriend, she is out shopping with friends or writing in her journal!

Clawdeen Wolf

Clawdeen Wolf is the daughter of the Werewolf. She's outspoken, independent, and has a no-nonsense attitude, but beneath her tough exterior, she's caring, generous, and fiercely loyal to her friends and family. Clawdeen loves fashion and enjoys designing clothes, experimenting with hairstyles and makeup, and shopping for the latest trends. She dreams of building her own fashion empire!

Lagoona Blue

Lagoona Blue is the daughter of the Sea Monster. She's easygoing, patient, and considerate, but isn't afraid to speak her mind or stand up for her friends. She loves sports, especially swimming! She's the captain of Monster High's swim team and loves studying oceanography because it allows her to learn more about the underwater world.

Cleo De Nile

Cleo De Nile is the daughter of the Mummy. Being Egyptian royality, she loves being popular, getting attention and making sure everything goes her way. She can be arrogant, dramatic, competitive, and demanding, but underneath her royal attitude, she has a caring side and is fiercely loyal to the people she loves. Cleo enjoys fashion, wearing jewelry, shopping, and maintaining her royal appearance. She is also the captain of Monster High's Fearleading Squad, ensuring her role as queen bee at the school.
